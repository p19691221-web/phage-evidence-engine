# Code of Conduct

PHAGE research depends on adversarial criticism conducted professionally.

- Attack claims, models, fixtures, and code — not people.
- State uncertainty and assumptions explicitly.
- Do not fabricate evidence or misrepresent source authority.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and security boundaries.
- Do not use the project to facilitate harmful operational activity.
- Treat `UNRESOLVED` as a legitimate research result.

Maintainers may remove contributions that expose sensitive information, make unsupported accusations, or materially exceed the project's research scope.
